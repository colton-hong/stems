### When the song lacks an instrument

Nothing extra. A stem far below the mix is delivered at once with its note and no other model is run for it (`gate.rules.retry_empty` is off: on held-out tracks a retry never rescued an instrument and sometimes delivered leftovers as one, `docs/gate-on-real-output.md`). Until 2026-11-24 such a stem was tried again with the fallback model, which for a 4-minute song on this Mac's GPU cost 26 s more without guitar, 11 s without piano and 36 s without both, by arithmetic on the model times above.

### The tagger

When `listening.enabled` is on, the tagger (`beats_iter3plus_as2m_cpt2`, on cpu) listens to the mix and to every attempt at every stem. Measured in the jobs of `runs/product/product-v1`, which ran real songs through the worker with six stems asked for:

| Song | Audio | Times it listened | Tagger seconds | Per minute of audio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `014f3712` | 344 s | 9 | 44 | 7.7 s |
| `01c8ba69` | 249 s | 8 | 30 | 7.3 s |
| `028c6f6a` | 240 s | 9 | 36 | 8.9 s |

At the median, a 4-minute song costs 31 s of tagger time, beside 69 s of separation in the default mode on the device of the table above (45% more). Those jobs shared the machine with a benchmark, so the figure may be on the high side. What it buys: the gate acts on nothing the tagger hears (`docs/gate-on-real-output.md`); its scores go into each stem's notes and to Jev. `listening.enabled` is **off** in `config/defaults.yaml`.

**What this costs on a rented GPU is not known.** Modal charges $0.000164 per second for a T4 and $0.000222 for an L4 (https://modal.com/pricing, read 2026-11-24; CPU and memory are charged on top, and non-preemptible runs at three times the list price). The cost of a song is that price times the seconds a T4 or L4 takes. Those seconds have not been measured: every timing in this repo is from Apple MPS or CPU, and a different GPU can be faster or slower by an amount nobody here has observed. One job on Modal (BLOCKED.md B4) gives the number.

## Storage and downloads

Cloudflare R2: $0.015 per GB-month, no charge for downloads, 10 GB-month free each month (https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/, read 2026-11-24).

One job keeps at most 0.83 GB for 7 days: the upload, six stems and a zip of them, all counted as 24-bit 44.1 kHz stereo WAV. That is arithmetic from the format, not a measurement, and it is the upper end: FLAC and MP3 are smaller. It comes to $0.002889 per song, before the free allowance.

## Not priced

- Amazon S3 Standard storage and internet egress prices: looked for at https://aws.amazon.com/s3/pricing/ and not found there.
- The machine the API runs on, and its database.
- Electricity and wear when the worker is this laptop.
- Azure: nothing has run there.
